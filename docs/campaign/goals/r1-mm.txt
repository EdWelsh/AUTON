Create kernel/include/mm.h, kernel/mm/pmm.c and kernel/mm/vmm.c implementing the interface in mm.md, including its REQUIRED sections (reserved regions, bitmap placement, reporting, boot handover, huge-page split, vmm_protect, out-of-memory unwinding, TLB invalidation). The interface the frozen host suite compiles against is in tests/kernel/mm_reference/include (already in this workspace; do not change it). Your headers under kernel/include must declare exactly what it declares, with the same names, types, constants and struct members; where the spec and that header differ, the header is what the suite builds against.
