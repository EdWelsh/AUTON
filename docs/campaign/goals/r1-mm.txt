Implement the physical and virtual memory managers specified in the mm subsystem (read it with read_spec mm; also read_spec arch/x86_64 for page-table bits). Create kernel/include/mm.h, kernel/mm/pmm.c and kernel/mm/vmm.c implementing the interface in mm.md, including its REQUIRED sections (reserved regions, bitmap placement, reporting, boot handover, huge-page split, vmm_protect, out-of-memory unwinding, TLB invalidation). Call pmm_init then vmm_init from kernel/boot/kernel_main.c and print the [MM] marker exactly as mm.md specifies. Do not change kernel/lib/phys.c.

The interface the frozen host suite compiles against is in tests/kernel/mm_reference/include (already in this workspace; do not change it). Your headers under kernel/include must declare exactly what it declares, with the same names, types, constants and struct members; where the spec and that header differ, the header is what the suite builds against.
The virtual-memory suite compiles against its own interface, tests/kernel/vmm_reference/include (also in this workspace; do not change it): vmm_*, vmm_host.h and that directory's mm.h. kernel/include/mm.h must satisfy both interfaces: the allocator's (mm_reference) and the VMM's, without defining anything twice (phys_to_virt in particular is defined once).
