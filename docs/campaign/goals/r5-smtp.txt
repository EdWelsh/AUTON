Implement the SMTP receive-and-store service. Read its specification with read_spec services/smtp. Write kernel/include/smtp.h exactly as its Interface section declares, and the implementation in kernel/services/smtp/smtp.c: the RFC 5321 commands, limits, relaying refusal, dot-stuffing, and storage on the FAT32 volume with the sequence recovered at boot, all as the REQUIRED sections say. Wire it into kernel_main with its markers.

The interface the frozen host suite compiles against is in tests/kernel/smtp_reference/include (already in this workspace; do not change it). Your headers under kernel/include must declare exactly what it declares, with the same names, types, constants and struct members; where the spec and that header differ, the header is what the suite builds against.
