Implement storage in this tree: a VirtIO block driver and a FAT32 filesystem. Read the driver with read_spec drivers (section "SLM-Managed Driver: VirtIO Block") and read_spec drivers/virtio-blk, and the filesystem with read_spec fs (section "FAT32"). Create kernel/include/blk.h and kernel/drivers/blk/virtio_blk.c (use dma_alloc from kernel/include/phys.h for the virtqueue), and kernel/include/fs.h, kernel/fs/vfs.c and kernel/fs/fat32.c. In kernel/boot/kernel_main.c, after the PCI scan, bring up the first virtio block device, mount it as FAT32, read /SEED.TXT, create /AUTON.TXT holding exactly the 16 bytes "written by AUTON", and print the [BLK] and [FS] markers exactly as virtio-blk.md and fs.md specify.

The interface the frozen host suite compiles against is in tests/kernel/fat32_reference/include (already in this workspace; do not change it). Your headers under kernel/include must declare exactly what it declares, with the same names, types, constants and struct members; where the spec and that header differ, the header is what the suite builds against.
